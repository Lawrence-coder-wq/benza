First public version. *Prima versione pubblica.*

**Use it / Usala:** https://lawrence-coder-wq.github.io/benza/

**On your phone / Sul telefono**
- Android (Chrome): open the link, tap **Installa** in the banner, or menu ⋮ → *Aggiungi a schermata Home*.
- iPhone (Safari): open the link, tap **Condividi** → *Aggiungi alla schermata Home*.
It opens full screen like an app, starts instantly, and if there is no signal it shows the last prices it downloaded, with their date.

**What it does / Cosa fa**
- The cheapest stations around you or around any Italian town, for petrol, diesel, LPG and methane, self-service or attended.
- Official prices from the Ministry's open data, refreshed every morning by a GitHub Action. Prices older than 10 days are hidden; stations with clearly wrong coordinates are dropped.
- How much you save on a 50-litre tank compared with the local average.
- No ads, no account, no tracking. Your position never leaves your device.

**Host it yourself / Per ospitarla da te:** download `benza-v1.0.0.zip`, unzip it on any static web server. Run `python strumenti/aggiorna.py` to refresh the prices.
